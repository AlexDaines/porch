# Custom styling must reach real content

The user requires custom styling on live content, with the restraint of Plural and Light Phone. A native shell around Instagram's unchanged website does not meet that requirement. Feed and Stories must be separate to limit simultaneous stimulation.

The intermediate injected stylesheet was superseded by the [native data client](2026-09-10-native-data-client.md). Its product requirement survives: the actual posts, stories and messages are now rendered in custom SwiftUI views.

Light's [developer principles](https://developers.thelightphone.com/) informed deliberate, purpose-bound interaction. Porch is an independent interpretation, with no affiliation.
